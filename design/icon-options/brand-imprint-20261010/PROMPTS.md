Put exactly THREE isolated, generous, compact black silhouettes in a simple horizontal row on pure white, with equal spacing and equal optical size. No titles, labels or caption text. Each symbol must occupy a compact nearly-square footprint, use very few broad shapes and remain clear at 48 pixels.
LEFT — a bespoke readable Chinese brand-glyph 未, five strong simple strokes, upper horizontal SHORTER than the second horizontal, one vertical stem, the left descending stroke and the right descending stroke. Keep the character unmistakeably 未, never 木 or 末. Shape the long lower-right stroke into one gently returning smooth curve, as a subtle idea of a memory coming back. One coherent silhouette, not a filing cabinet, not a treasure chest with latch, no lock. Represents a moment safely kept for later.

## 字标精修稿

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one finished square launcher-icon concept for a Chinese personal-memory app named 未完成 (Unfinished). The app saves lived moments and allows a later rediscovery; it is private, gentle and not a task-completion tool.
Selected subject after silhouette screening: ONE original Chinese brand glyph. Text (verbatim): "未". Render exactly this one character, NOT the entire name. It must be immediately readable as 未. Five strokes: short upper horizontal, longer second horizontal, central vertical rising above the upper stroke, lower-left diagonal and lower-right diagonal. Do not accidentally make 木 or 末.
Design direction: a bespoke warm humanist typographic imprint, quietly distinctive rather than a stock font. Broad confident strokes with softly rounded edges, clean curves and subtle tapering at the two lower terminals. The lower-right stroke gently sweeps outward and curls upward a little like a returning current, but must remain a recognisable right descending stroke of 未. The other strokes stay calm and stable. Both horizontal strokes have a slight organic warmth; no artificial break or floating dot. This is a unified single glyph, not words sitting on a beach.
Style and palette: sharply defined warm ivory glyph on a full-square muted dark mineral blue-green ground. Use only two flat solid colours, ivory approximately #F2EADD and ground approximately #315966. No texture, bevel, gradients, glass or shadows. The distinction should come from the tailored letterform, not effects.
Composition: a compact, balanced nearly-square glyph, carefully centered with generous breathing room. The entire meaningful glyph must fit inside the centered 60 percent square of the artwork, suitable for the Android 66/108 safe zone. Make the strokes sufficiently broad to remain clear at 48px. Fill the background to the edges; do not pre-mask it.
Mood: a lasting private imprint of a life still unfolding. Calm, warm, restrained, confident, recognisable after seeing it once.
Constraints: one glyph only; no additional text, letters, captions, numbers, icon label, frame, photograph, notebook, bottle, boat, clock, sun, cloud, arrow, checkmark, surrounding wave ornaments, detached fragments, decorative fine lines, 3D object, mockup or rounded-square outer outline. Output only the single square icon artwork.
